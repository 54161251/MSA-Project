# Strata Forge S1 FDM 3D Printer: User Guide

Fictional manual written for the MSA demo. Strata is not a real company.

[p.1]
## Specifications
- Build volume: 256 mm x 256 mm x 256 mm
- Footprint: 0.5 m x 0.55 m
- Nozzle temperature: up to 300 C
- Bed temperature: up to 110 C
- Power: 120 V, 350 W
- Filament diameter: 1.75 mm

[p.2]
## Safety warnings
- The nozzle reaches 300 C and the bed 110 C. Do not touch either while the printer is hot.
- Keep fingers away from the moving print head and belts while printing.
- Do not leave a print running overnight without staff approval.
- Printing ABS releases fumes; only print ABS with the enclosure fan on.

## Personal protective equipment
- Do not touch the nozzle or bed until they have cooled below 50 C.
- Use the scraper, not your fingers, to remove prints.

[p.3]
## Before you start
- Check the build plate is clean and seated on its magnets.
- Check there is enough filament on the spool for the job.
- Confirm your filament is on the approved list.

## Operation
- Slice your model with the shop profile for this printer.
- Load the file from the USB drive and start the print.
- Watch the first layer; cancel if it does not stick.
- When the print finishes, let the bed cool, then remove the plate and flex it to release the part.

[p.4]
## Materials
- Allowed: PLA filament.
- Allowed: PETG filament.
- Allowed: TPU filament.
- Ask staff: ABS filament, only with the enclosure fan on.
- Never: nylon or carbon-fiber filled filament. They need a hardened nozzle this printer does not have.

[p.5]
## Maintenance
- Every 50 hours of use: clean the nozzle and check for clogs.
- Every 30 days: lubricate the Z-axis lead screw.
- Every 90 days: check belt tension.

## Troubleshooting
- Print does not stick: clean the plate with isopropyl alcohol and re-level.
- Stringing: lower the nozzle temperature by 5 C.

[p.6]
## Emergency
- If you see smoke or smell burning plastic, press the power switch off and tell staff.
- For a burn, cool the skin under cool running water for 20 minutes and tell staff.
