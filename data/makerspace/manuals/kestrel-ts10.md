# Kestrel TS10 Cabinet Table Saw: Safety and Operation Manual

Fictional manual written for the MSA demo. Kestrel is not a real company.

[p.1]
## Specifications
- Blade: 254 mm (10 in), 40-tooth general purpose
- Footprint: 1.0 m x 0.8 m
- Motor: 2.2 kW
- Power: 240 V, 15 A
- Rip capacity: 760 mm

[p.2]
## Safety warnings
- Kickback can throw wood toward you at high speed. Always use the riving knife and blade guard.
- Never reach over or behind the spinning blade.
- Never cut freehand. Always use the fence or the miter gauge, never both at once.
- Use a push stick whenever your hand would come within 15 cm of the blade.
- Stand to one side of the blade, out of the kickback path.

## Personal protective equipment
- Wear safety glasses.
- Wear hearing protection.
- No gloves, loose sleeves, jewelry or long loose hair near the blade.

[p.3]
## Before you start
- Check the blade guard, riving knife and anti-kickback pawls are fitted.
- Set the blade height about 6 mm above the stock.
- Check the stock for nails, screws and knots.
- Clear the table and the area around the saw.

## Operation
- Turn on dust collection, then the saw, and let the blade reach full speed.
- Feed the stock steadily with a push stick, keeping it against the fence.
- Turn off the saw and wait for the blade to stop before removing offcuts.

[p.4]
## Materials
- Allowed: softwood, hardwood, plywood and MDF.
- Never: metal of any kind.
- Never: acrylic or other plastics with the standard blade.
- Ask staff: any stock shorter than 300 mm or narrower than 50 mm.

[p.5]
## Maintenance
- Every 7 days: check that the blade guard and riving knife are aligned with the blade.
- Every 30 days: clean pitch from the blade and table.
- Every 90 days: check the fence is parallel to the blade.

## Troubleshooting
- Burning: the blade is dull or the fence is not parallel; tell staff.

[p.6]
## Emergency
- Press the paddle switch off for any problem.
- For any cut, apply firm pressure with a clean cloth and call 911 for deep cuts.
