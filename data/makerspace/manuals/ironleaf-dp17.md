# Ironleaf DP17 Floor Drill Press: Instruction Manual

Fictional manual written for the MSA demo. Ironleaf is not a real company.

[p.1]
## Specifications
- Swing: 430 mm (17 in)
- Footprint: 0.6 m x 0.45 m
- Speeds: 12, from 210 to 3,600 rpm
- Power: 120 V, 3/4 HP

[p.2]
## Safety warnings
- Clamp the work to the table. A spinning part can hit your hand.
- Remove the chuck key before starting the drill.
- Never hold small parts by hand; use a drill press vise.

## Personal protective equipment
- Wear safety glasses.
- No gloves, loose sleeves, jewelry or long loose hair near the chuck.

[p.3]
## Before you start
- Pick the right speed for your bit and material from the chart on the machine.
- Clamp your work and put scrap wood underneath it.

## Operation
- Lower the bit slowly and lift it often to clear chips.
- Turn off the drill and wait for the chuck to stop before removing the part.

[p.4]
## Materials
- Allowed: wood, plywood and MDF.
- Allowed: acrylic at low speed.
- Allowed: aluminum and mild steel with cutting fluid.

[p.5]
## Maintenance
- Every 30 days: clean the table and lubricate the quill.
- Every 180 days: check the belt for wear.

## Troubleshooting
- Bit wanders: use a center punch or pilot hole first.

[p.6]
## Emergency
- Turn the drill off with the red switch.
- If clothing is caught, hit the switch and call for help; do not pull.
