# Cutline C24 Vinyl Cutter: Quick Start and Safety Guide

Fictional manual written for the MSA demo. Cutline is not a real company.

[p.1]
## Specifications
- Cutting width: 610 mm (24 in)
- Footprint: 0.9 m x 0.4 m (on stand)
- Blade: 45 degree carbide
- Power: 120 V, 40 W

[p.2]
## Safety warnings
- The blade is very sharp. Never touch the blade tip.
- Keep fingers clear of the pinch rollers while the cutter moves.

## Personal protective equipment
- Use the weeding hook, not a craft knife, to remove vinyl.

[p.3]
## Before you start
- Load the vinyl so the pinch rollers sit over the white marks.
- Run a test cut in a corner before the full job.

## Operation
- Send the design from the shop computer.
- Lift the pinch rollers and unload the vinyl when the job ends.

[p.4]
## Materials
- Allowed: adhesive vinyl.
- Allowed: heat transfer vinyl.
- Allowed: paper and cardstock with the paper blade.

[p.5]
## Maintenance
- Every 90 days: replace the blade and clean the pinch rollers.

## Troubleshooting
- Cuts do not go through: raise the blade force by 10 g.

[p.6]
## Emergency
- For a cut, apply pressure with a clean cloth and tell staff.
