# Lumenic Vector 60 CO2 Laser Cutter: Owner's Manual

Fictional manual written for the MSA demo. Lumenic is not a real company.

[p.1]
## Specifications
- Laser type: sealed CO2 tube
- Laser power: 60 W
- Work area: 900 mm x 600 mm
- Footprint: 1.45 m x 0.95 m
- Maximum material thickness: 10 mm
- Power: 120 V, 15 A dedicated circuit
- Exhaust: 150 mm duct, external fan required
- Laser class: Class 1 with lid closed and interlocks working

[p.2]
## Safety warnings
- The laser can start a fire in seconds. Never leave the laser unattended while it is running.
- Keep the lid closed while the laser is firing. The lid interlock stops the beam when opened.
- Never disable, tape over or bypass the lid interlock.
- Run the exhaust fan and air assist for every job to remove smoke and fumes.
- Keep a CO2 fire extinguisher within reach of the machine.
- Do not look at the cutting area through the lid if the viewing window is cracked or missing.

## Personal protective equipment
- Never leave the laser unattended: stay at the machine and watch the whole job.
- Wear safety glasses when cleaning the lens or mirrors.
- Tie back long hair and loose clothing when loading material.

[p.3]
## Before you start
- Check the honeycomb bed is clean and free of scraps, which can catch fire.
- Confirm the exhaust fan and air assist are switched on.
- Check the lens is clean; a dirty lens causes flare-ups.
- Confirm your material is on the approved list for this machine.
- Know where the fire extinguisher and fire blanket are.

## Operation
- Place the material flat on the bed and close the lid.
- Set focus height with the focus gauge.
- Run a frame preview before cutting to check the job fits the material.
- Start the job and watch it until it finishes.
- If a flame lasts more than a second, press pause and wait for it to go out.
- After the job ends, wait 30 seconds for smoke to clear before opening the lid.

[p.4]
## Materials
- Allowed: cast and extruded acrylic up to 10 mm.
- Allowed: plywood and MDF up to 6 mm.
- Allowed: hardwood up to 6 mm.
- Allowed: cardboard and paper.
- Allowed: vegetable-tanned leather up to 3 mm.
- Allowed: cotton fabric and felt.
- Allowed: polycarbonate up to 1 mm, for engraving only.
- Never: PVC or vinyl. Cutting it releases chlorine gas that harms lungs and destroys the optics.
- Never: ABS. It melts and releases toxic fumes.
- Never: HDPE or polystyrene foam. They melt and catch fire.
- Never: fiberglass or carbon fiber. The resin binder releases toxic fumes.
- Ask staff: any material not listed here, or anything with an unknown coating.

[p.5]
## Maintenance
- Every 8 hours of use: clean the focus lens with lens wipes.
- Every 40 hours of use: clean the three mirrors and check alignment.
- Every 30 days: empty and clean the honeycomb bed and scrap tray.
- Every 30 days: check the chiller water level and top up with distilled water.
- Every 180 days: inspect the exhaust duct for grease and debris.

## Troubleshooting
- Cuts are weaker than usual: clean the lens and mirrors, then check focus.
- Laser does not fire: check the lid is fully closed and the chiller is running.
- Lots of flame: lower the power, raise the speed, and check air assist.

[p.6]
## Emergency
- If a fire does not go out on its own within a few seconds, keep the lid closed, press the emergency stop and use the CO2 extinguisher.
- If the fire spreads beyond the machine, leave the room and call 911.
- Report every flare-up to staff, even small ones.
