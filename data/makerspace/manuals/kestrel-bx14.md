# Kestrel BX14 Bandsaw: Operating Instructions

Fictional manual written for the MSA demo. Kestrel is not a real company.

[p.1]
## Specifications
- Wheel size: 355 mm (14 in)
- Footprint: 0.7 m x 0.6 m
- Cutting height: 150 mm
- Power: 120 V, 1 HP

[p.2]
## Safety warnings
- Set the upper blade guard about 6 mm above the stock.
- Never back out of a curved cut while the blade is moving.
- Keep fingers out of the line of the blade; use a push stick near the blade.

## Personal protective equipment
- Wear safety glasses.
- No gloves, loose sleeves or jewelry.

[p.3]
## Before you start
- Check blade tension and tracking.
- Check the blade is the right width for your curves.

## Operation
- Start the saw and let it reach full speed.
- Feed the stock slowly and keep it flat on the table.
- Make relief cuts before cutting tight curves.

[p.4]
## Materials
- Allowed: softwood, hardwood, plywood and MDF.
- Allowed: acrylic with the fine-tooth blade fitted by staff.
- Never: metal with the wood blade.

[p.5]
## Maintenance
- Every 30 days: clean the tires and check the blade for cracks.
- Every 90 days: check the guide blocks and bearings.

## Troubleshooting
- Blade wanders: lower the guide and check tension.

[p.6]
## Emergency
- Turn the saw off with the paddle switch.
- If the blade breaks, step away and wait for the wheels to stop, then tell staff.
