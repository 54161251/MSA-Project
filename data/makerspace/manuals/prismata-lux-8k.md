# Prismata Lux 8K Resin Printer: Operating Manual

Fictional manual written for the MSA demo. Prismata is not a real company.

[p.1]
## Specifications
- Build volume: 218 mm x 123 mm x 220 mm
- Footprint: 0.3 m x 0.3 m
- Light source: 405 nm UV LED array
- Power: 120 V, 72 W
- Resin vat capacity: 600 ml

[p.2]
## Safety warnings
- Uncured resin irritates skin and eyes and can cause allergic reactions over time.
- Work only in the ventilated resin area with the extraction fan on.
- Never pour resin or used isopropyl alcohol down a drain.
- Keep uncured resin and isopropyl alcohol away from heat and open flames.

## Personal protective equipment
- Wear nitrile gloves whenever you handle resin, the vat or uncured prints.
- Wear safety glasses when pouring resin or washing prints.
- Wear a long-sleeved top or lab coat.

[p.3]
## Before you start
- Turn on the extraction fan in the resin area.
- Check the vat film for scratches or holes.
- Stir the resin bottle gently before pouring.

## Operation
- Pour resin into the vat up to the max line.
- Start the print from the touchscreen and close the UV cover.
- Wash finished prints in isopropyl alcohol for 3 minutes.
- Cure prints in the UV curing station before handling them without gloves.

[p.4]
## Materials
- Allowed: standard resin from the shop supply.
- Ask staff: tough, flexible or castable resins.
- Never: resin you brought from home without staff approval.

[p.5]
## Maintenance
- Every 14 days: filter leftover resin back into its bottle and clean the vat.
- Every 30 days: inspect and replace the vat film if cloudy or scratched.
- Every 7 days: change the isopropyl alcohol in the wash station.

## Troubleshooting
- Print stuck to the film: remove it with the plastic scraper only, wearing gloves.

[p.6]
## Emergency
- Resin on skin: wash with soap and water right away.
- Resin in eyes: rinse at the eyewash station for 15 minutes and call 911.
- Spills: wipe up with paper towels while wearing gloves and put them in the resin waste bin.
