# Solderix SX-70 Soldering Station: User Manual

Fictional manual written for the MSA demo. Solderix is not a real company.

[p.1]
## Specifications
- Temperature range: 200 C to 480 C
- Footprint: 0.45 m x 0.35 m (station, stand and fume extractor)
- Power: 120 V, 70 W

[p.2]
## Safety warnings
- The tip reaches 480 C. Always return the iron to its stand.
- Solder fumes irritate the lungs. Run the fume extractor every time.
- Never solder on a circuit that is plugged in or powered.
- Never solder directly onto lithium battery cells.

## Personal protective equipment
- Wear safety glasses; solder can spit.
- Turn on the fume extractor before heating the iron.
- Wash your hands after soldering, especially with leaded solder.

[p.3]
## Before you start
- Check the iron cord for damage.
- Wet the cleaning sponge or use the brass wool.

## Operation
- Set the temperature to 350 C for lead-free solder.
- Heat the joint, then feed solder into the joint, not the iron.
- Turn the station off when you leave the bench.

[p.4]
## Materials
- Allowed: lead-free solder.
- Ask staff: leaded solder, only at the marked bench.
- Never: soldering to lithium battery cells.

[p.5]
## Maintenance
- Every 30 days: replace the fume extractor filter.
- Every 90 days: inspect and replace worn tips.

## Troubleshooting
- Solder will not stick: clean and re-tin the tip.

[p.6]
## Emergency
- For a burn, cool the skin under cool running water for 20 minutes and tell staff.
- If anything catches fire, turn off the station and use the extinguisher.
