# Thimble & Co. T900 Sewing Machine: Instruction Book

Fictional manual written for the MSA demo. Thimble & Co. is not a real company.
(This manual leaves out the machine's footprint on purpose, to show the Intake agent
flagging a missing spec.)

[p.1]
## Specifications
- Stitches: 90 built-in
- Maximum speed: 850 stitches per minute
- Power: 120 V, 65 W

[p.2]
## Safety warnings
- Keep fingers away from the needle area while sewing.
- Turn the machine off before changing the needle or presser foot.

## Personal protective equipment
- Tie back long hair and keep loose sleeves away from the needle.

[p.3]
## Before you start
- Check the needle is straight and the right size for the fabric.
- Wind and insert the bobbin.

## Operation
- Lower the presser foot before you start sewing.
- Guide the fabric gently; do not pull it.

[p.4]
## Materials
- Allowed: cotton fabric, felt, canvas and denim.
- Ask staff: leather and faux leather, which need a special needle.

[p.5]
## Maintenance
- Every 90 days: clean lint from the bobbin area and oil the hook.

## Troubleshooting
- Thread bunching: re-thread the top thread with the presser foot up.

[p.6]
## Emergency
- For a needle injury, turn the machine off, do not pull the fabric, and call staff.
